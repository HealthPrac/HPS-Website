Who we are

Built from inside the work — where care and business discipline meet.

HealthPrac Solutions was built from real experience in healthcare operations, compliance, regulation, business performance, and implementation. We understand both the realities of care and the discipline required to sustain it.

That understanding holds whether you lead an independent practice, a growing practice, or a multi-site care organisation — and whether the question in front of you is a regulator, a roster, a board paper, or the long arc of sustainable growth.

We help healthcare leaders gain a clearer view of the health of their business — without compromising care, compliance, or operational integrity.

Why we built this

The platform exists because we kept rebuilding the same systems for organisation after organisation. After the third or fourth time, we wrote it as software — then assembled the partnership team that puts it to work in the kind of environment we came from.

We are deliberately small, deliberately senior, and deliberately patient about the operators we work with. HealthPrac is what we wish we had had — on the days the binder was empty and the regulator was already on the road.

Who we serve

Wherever you are in the business of healthcare — we can be of service.

01

Solo Practitioner

An independent GP, specialist, or allied health professional who needs the back-office running cleanly — compliance, billing, HR, and governance — without a full administrative team behind them.

  • Practice compliance & quality management
  • Billing, revenue cycle & financial visibility
  • HR, credentialing & locum management
  • Executive reporting for board or accreditation
02

Multi-site Group Practice

A group of practices — two sites or twenty — where the challenge is consistency, visibility, and governance across locations without drowning the centre in manual reporting and fragmented systems.

  • Multi-site operational oversight on one page
  • Standardised QMS and incident management
  • Consolidated workforce & payroll governance
  • Group finance and performance reporting
03

Hospital & Day Clinic

A hospital or day surgery where operational complexity, regulatory pressure, and workforce scale demand systems that are defensible, audit-ready, and visible to the board — not reconstructed from spreadsheets at month end.

  • Governance, audit readiness & regulatory compliance
  • Workforce, rostering & credentialing at scale
  • Incident management & quality systems
  • Executive command centre & KPI dashboard
04

Care Facility & Senior Living

A residential care home, retirement village, or senior living group where every day involves residents, families, regulators, and staff — and where clinical governance, safety, and dignity are non-negotiable.

  • Resident management & care governance
  • Family portal & communication
  • Safety, incident & medication management
  • Regulatory compliance & accreditation readiness
